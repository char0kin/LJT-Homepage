Who I Am

I am Junteng Liu, a first-year PhD candidate at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST) specializing in natural language processing (NLP) and machine learning. My research focuses on improving the reliability and interpretability of large language models (LLMs), particularly addressing issues of hallucination and truthfulness in vision-language models.
